# Starkeeper

EN: the melee enemy that comes after the Starweaver: a flying rig puppet (hood and face drawn as one piece, a long cloak, one shoe sprite on both feet). Folder layout follows the other entities: `Prefab/` (the prefab), `Script/` (stat, skill, behavior, combat data). No `Art/` yet: the puppet is PNGs in `StreamingAssets/BaseContent/visual/starkeeper/`.

- **Content (data):** `characters/StarkeeperCharacter.json` (`project_n:starkeeper`: stats, `body.prefab` `starkeeper`, `body.visual` `project_n:starkeeper`, `body.build` `normal`) and the puppet in `visual/starkeeper.json` + `visual/starkeeper/*.png`, written by `ArtSource/VisualDemo/export_starkeeper.py` (reads the demo through `starweaver_layout.js` and `sample_starkeeper.js`; never hand-edit the numbers). The character JSON is hand-written.
- **Prefab:** `Prefab/Starkeeper.prefab`, catalog key `project_n:starkeeper` in `Assets/Resources/PNEntities/PNEntityPrefabs.asset`. Made from the Starweaver prefab (same body, rigidbody, capsule, `PNEnemyMove`, `PNVisualBody`), with `PNStarkeeperStat`, `PNStarkeeperSkill` and `PNStarkeeperBehavior` (behavior id `starkeeper`) in place of the Starweaver's, and no needle.
- **Scripts:** `PNStarkeeperStat : PNEnemyStat` and `PNStarkeeperSkill : PNEnemySkill` are empty extension points (phase policy goes in the skill, as `PNSentinelSkill` does). `PNStarkeeperBehavior : PNEnemyMeleeBehavior` runs the shared melee state machine and casts `PNStarkeeperCombatContent.Slash`.
- **PLACEHOLDERS (not designed yet):** the stats (HP 2500, Lorica 500, ATK 120, DEF 15, move speed 1.4) and the slash (120% attack power, physical, 1.5 s cooldown, instant damage, no projectile/VFX, no attack motion: the motion set has no `attack` state yet). Real moveset, numbers and attack motions replace these.
- **Puppet:** the humanoid head and face are `hide`n in the appearance; `sk_head` carries its own face. The cloak is two bendable strips (`cloakB` back, `cloakF` front, 14 segments), the hood bone rocks a little. Motion states: idle = hover, walk = fly, run = fly_fast, hurt (overlay, no leg tracks), dead (standing slump), stun (standing slump, Lorica break), recover (authored in `starkeeper_actions.py`). The legs stay under the cloak: stun/dead are slumps, not falls.
- **Scene:** `Starkeeper Spawn` (a `PNSceneEnemySpawn`, `project_n:starkeeper`) in ErMode at x = 2, next to the Starweaver's at x = -2.

VI: kẻ địch cận chiến sau Starweaver, bay, đầu và mặt vẽ một mảnh, áo choàng dài. Số liệu và đòn đánh hiện là **tạm** (chưa thiết kế). Khi di chuyển prefab phải kèm `.meta` để giữ GUID mà catalog tham chiếu.
